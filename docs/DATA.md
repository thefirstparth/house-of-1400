# DATA

Every live value goes through `/api/live/<key>`. Each returns `{ok, value, source, as_of, stale}`.
Rule: primary, then backup, then the edition snapshot (with its time), otherwise hide the field. Never zero, never blank, never a guess.

## Tests
`npm test` hits every live function and asserts shape, freshness and sane ranges (for example Sensex between 40,000 and 150,000; Bengaluru temperature between 5 and 45). The daily run executes it before publishing and records failures in the edition snapshot, not in the paper.

Tennis next matches (`tennis_players`, lib/trial.js): ESPN's ATP scoreboard plus Tennis Explorer's player pages; used by the daily run (validated) and, from 30 Sep 2026, live on the page every five minutes (cache 5 min).

The Crease (`crease`, lib/cricket.js + lib/crease-live.js, from 1 Oct 2026): Cricbuzz's India team schedule and results pages (the matchInfo objects in their page data), grouped into the next match, the series under way, anything else in the next ten days, and the next tour. Cache 10 minutes. Fails: the page shows the edition's rows.

### Leads (from 1 Oct 2026)
`/api/live/wire` also returns `leads`: items from feeds of `kind: "lead"` (The New York Times: Technology, Science, Well; The Athletic: football, Real Madrid, F1, tennis, Warriors), kept out of the day's items and stories. For each (the newest five per lead feed, 30 at most), Google News is searched for the story's most telling words (the name in its summary when it has one, quoted) over seven days; results from `sources.paywalled` outlets and hosts and from `sources.not_outlets` (social networks, aggregators) are dropped, and a result counts only if it carries the whole name and two more of the headline's words, or else half the headline's words (at least three, by stem). National outlets first, up to three. The searches stop 20 seconds into the call (`elsewhere: null`). NYT's RSS terms allow personal, non-commercial use; the paper never reprints its text or images.

- **f1_market** (from 1 Oct 2026): the next race's winner as the markets price it, for Paddock's race preview printed at 14:00. Kalshi `trade-api/v2/events?series_ticker=KXF1RACE&status=open`, matched by race name ("Bahrain Grand Prix in Malaysia" matches "bahrain"), then that event's markets: mid of bid and ask when the spread is 5 cents or less, else the last price; top six. Polymarket's race-winner event too when one exists. Cached 30 minutes. In the snapshot; the page never shows it live.
- **odds** (from the 3 Oct 2026 edition; Parth: "every upcoming match, found automatically"): `lib/odds.js`. Every open match market on Kalshi (the series in config `betting.odds.kalshi`: T20, ODI and Test cricket; La Liga, Champions League, Copa del Rey, Nations League, friendlies and World Cup qualifiers; ATP; NBA; the F1 race and qualifying) and Polymarket (the tags in `betting.odds.polymarket`, a match's own event only, slug ending in its date), from yesterday to 15 days ahead, whose sides include a followed team or player (India's cricket team, the club and the national sides, the tennis players, the NBA team; F1 is the whole grid). Kalshi prices are the middle of a bid and ask 5 cents or less apart, else the last trade; a ticker's time is New York's (`-26OCT030030` is 04:30 UTC). A match on both is shown once, from the one with more traded; markets under `min_volume` ($1,000) traded, decided (99% or more) or closed are left out; a match's chances are scaled to add up to 100. At most four Kalshi calls at a time, one retry on a 429; about 30 calls a refresh. The page matches each market to its own rows by sport, the two sides (the same name rules as the server: "Alcaraz" is "Carlos Alcaraz", "India" is not "India A" or "India women") and the date, a day either way; for a club or NBA side one matching side and the day are enough. It shows on The Fixture List, Madridismo's next four, The Wider Pitch's internationals, The Crease's series cards (the favourite only), Deuce's next matches and the Warriors' next games; a finished match shows none. In the snapshot from the 3 Oct run; the validator warns from 4 Oct when it is missing.
- **rain** (from 3 Oct 2026; Parth: "use the rain data from Weather Union, especially for Bengaluru", "I think the data is accurate", "6 calls every 15 minutes"): `lib/rain.js`, config `weather.rain`. Six gauges in three places: Home, Hoodi (Mahadevapura ZWL007643, KR Puram ZWL006369, Brookefields ZWL004341), Office, Koramangala (ZWL001156), Central (MG Road ZWL001962, Indiranagar ZWL001273). Per gauge `rain_intensity` (mm a minute) and `rain_accumulation` (mm since midnight); a place is raining when any of its gauges reads rain now (no second gauge needed, Parth's call), at the heaviest gauge's rate (x60 for mm an hour, in the IMD's words: light under 2.5, moderate under 10, heavy under 50, then very heavy), with the wettest gauge's total today. Weather Union gives no reading time, so the reading is as of the fetch; the page shows nothing from a reading over 45 minutes old. Cached 15 minutes (s-maxage 900), so at most 576 calls a day against the free key's 1,000. Tested 3 Oct: about a minute fresh; its temperatures run 1 to 1.5° warm and some anemometers are wrong, so only rain is used. Shown in Sky & Streets under Bengaluru (all three places, always), in Page One's "Rain" reading in place of the forecast's chance when a place is raining ("Rain now moderate, Home") or has had rain today ("Rain 6.4 mm today, home"), and in the wordmark's ink (rain at home or in the centre makes it rain when the model says it is dry). In the snapshot.
- **outlook** (from 1 Oct 2026): Sky & Streets' weeks and months for each city in config `weather`. Open-Meteo forecast with `past_days=7&forecast_days=14` (weeks: mean high, low, feels-like, humidity; rainy days measured as 2.5 mm or more, ahead as a 60% chance or more); Open-Meteo air quality (week mean US AQI, needs 48 hours); Open-Meteo seasonal (ECMWF SEAS5, 51 members, `past_days=31&forecast_days=93`): monthly medians of the members' means and totals, minus last month's error against `config/climate.json`. Cached 3 hours. A city that fails is left out; Bengaluru failing fails the key.
