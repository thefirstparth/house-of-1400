# The new design (v2), the default

Live on the real site since 1 Oct 2026; **the default for everyone since 4 Oct 2026** (Parth: "can we now push it to main now?"). The old design (v1) stays one tap away for a while.

- **Open it:** plain `/` opens v2. **The old design:** `/?v1`, or "The old design" in any v2 footer; the device remembers it until `/?v2`. Poster links (`/?poster=...`, the 16:00 poster run) stay in v1, so the posters do not change.
- **Where it applies:** the edition pages only (`/`, `/today`, `/e/<date>`). Archive, the editor's page and posters stay in v1. Desks are addressed `#d-news`, `#d-sport` and so on. A change of desk adds a history entry, so Back and Forward step through the desks (20 Oct); a jump within a desk keeps the desk in the address, and the address is read lowercased.
- **v1 is untouched.** `public/app.js` and `public/styles.css` are not changed by v2. The build (`scripts/build.mjs`) assembles `/v2.js` and `/v2.css` from them (`v2/assemble.mjs`), and `index.html` writes either v1's own tags, unchanged, or v2's (tests/v2.test.mjs). If a v2 patch no longer finds its anchor in `app.js` (after a change to the renderer), the build warns and ships v1 alone: the paper never fails to build because of v2.
- **Same paper, same data:** every section is drawn by the paper's own renderer from the same edition and the same live functions, with the same fallbacks and "as of" lines. Nothing the daily run writes changes.

## Files
- `layer.js` also draws the Fixture List (`fixturesV2`, from 10 Oct: design/fixtures-v3, 3b).
- `layer.js`: the page around the sections: Page One, the desks (config `desks_v2`), the masthead, tabs, footers, the lead in its section, The market expects (config `betting.page_one`), Money's 1D and open/closed line, the Fixture List's sport groups.
- `wordmark.js`: the halftone "1400" with the day in dots (design/page-one/README.md, FINAL).
- `v2.css`: over the live stylesheet, from the desk mocks (design/desks) and the reviewed sample (design/sample).
- `head.html`: the run line, the masthead and the tabs.
- `fonts/`: Newsreader, Source Serif 4, Libre Franklin, Playfair Display, UnifrakturMaguntia, Titillium Web (Fontsource woff2, OFL; licences beside them). Self-hosted: v2 never loads Google Fonts.
- `assemble.mjs`: the patch list and the switch. `shot.mjs`: QA (see below).

## QA
**Phones (Parth reads half the time on a phone: Nothing Phone 2, iPhone 17, 16 Pro and other iPhones):** `PHONES=nothing2,iphone17,iphone16pro,iphone16,iphone17promax,iphone16plus,iphone13,iphonemini,iphonese,iphone17land,nothing2land node v2/shot.mjs <tag> v2` shoots every desk on each at its own width and pixel density, with the height left by the browser's bars, light and dark. Phone checks measure against the screen's own width (a phone browser widens the page silently to fit anything that overflows), type under 11px, and links or buttons under 32px tall. 2 Oct: 154 pages, no errors, nothing wider than the screen, no clipped tables, no overlapping text; every small link has a hit area of about 40px (the masthead's two rows 30px, so they never overlap). Chromium only: Safari itself is not in this sandbox, which is one reason the wordmark no longer depends on font loading.

`npm run build`, then `node v2/shot.mjs <tag> v2` (every desk) or `node v2/shot.mjs <tag> v1`, optionally with a path (`/e/2026-09-25`) and `SIZES=1440x900-light,390x844-dark`. It serves `dist/`, answers the live calls from the edition's press-time snapshot and holds the clock at 15:00 IST; it reports page errors, sideways scroll, overlapping text, tables wider than their column and whether Page One fits one screen. On 1 Oct: v1 before and after these changes identical except the pulsing live dots; v2 clean on all desks at 390, 1280, 1440 and 1920, light and dark. On 2 Oct also at 1470x830 (a MacBook Air's window), 2560x1440 and 3440x1440: Page One one screen with all seven lines at each.

## The Glass look (behind /?glass, from 20 Oct 2026)
Parth picked Direction 10, "Glass, Swiss-tuned", from the redesign mockups and asked to try it on the real paper before deciding (DECISIONS.md, 20 Oct). It is a look over v2, not a new design: the same blocks, order, navigation, live data and behaviours.
- **The switch:** `/?glass` turns it on and the device remembers it (localStorage `h1400-look` = `glass`); `/?glass=off` (also `OFF`, `false`, `no`, `0`; or "Leave the Glass look" in the look's foot, which keeps the edition and desk) turns it off; `?v1` still wins. A script just before `</head>` (`GLASS` in assemble.mjs, after the saved Night/Day choice) sets `class="look-glass"` and `data-theme="light"` on `<html>` before the first paint. One mid-tone theme, no night: the Night/Auto buttons are hidden and `setTheme` keeps it light.
- **Files:** `glass.css`, appended to `/v2.css` after `v2.css`; every rule starts with `html.look-glass` (tests/glass.test.mjs checks it), so without the class the page is v2 to the pixel. In `layer.js`, behind `GLASS`: the small nameplate and the live figures (`#now`) move into the desk bar, `setTheme`/`followSun` stay light, and the foot carries "Back to the current design". The two nameplate lookups that said `#dtop .np` now say `.np` (there is one either way).
- **Page One:** still fitted by fitOne/balanceOne, which measure the cards as they are. On a short laptop window (under 900px tall) the look is a little more compact, and more so under 780px. Zoom at 1470x830: 0.89 (v2 0.93); 1440x900: 0.91 (0.99); 1920x1080: 1.11. On windows 720px tall or less it can print fewer of the day in a minute's lines than v2 (the usual fallback, never below five).
- **Drawings (20 Oct, Parth's notes):** never cropped in this look, always whole at the file's shape. On a wide screen (900px and up) each story card with a drawing beside its text is balanced by `glassArt` in layer.js: it tries the drawing at 62% down to 30% of the card (never past 820px on screen, or the file's width / 1.4), measures the text column at each with the drawing stepped out (`g-meas`), and keeps the width (`--g-artf`) at which the two are nearest the same height, with the headline beside the drawing or across the card (`g-head`) when that leaves clearly less empty space. Text still more than 1.35 times the drawing's height makes the card flow (`g-flow`): headline across, the drawing heading the first of two columns (`--g-cols`, one under 1000px of card). It runs on every desk fit, when fonts arrive, on Read more/less and when a drawing lands (a MutationObserver on `#main`).
- **The bar (20 Oct):** Page One's nameplate is centred; the desks' track fills the bar's free width, the desks spread along it (`justify-content:space-between`); Page One's tab always reads "Page One" (the pinned day-in-dots mark is v2's only).
- **The bar fits itself (QA panel, 20 Oct):** `glassBar` in layer.js, on every shell paint, resize and desk fit: the live figures drop, in order, the sky's word (`g-fit1`), the city's name (`g-fit2`), the temperature (`g-fit3`), the Sensex's change (`g-fit4`) and last "Sensex" (`g-fit5`); if the desks still would not fit on one row, the bar takes two rows (`g-two`, always on a phone). With two rows, the pinned bar sticks at `6px - --g-row1` so only the desks stay on screen. `--g-barh` (the bar's visible height) sets every jump's `scroll-margin-top`. The phone's tab row starts at the current tab (`tabsHome`, measured from the track in Glass) and its edge fade never covers it.
- **Page One (QA panel):** cards end level (`g-level`, added after balanceOne measures); tighter under 900px and 780px tall so every day-in-a-minute line stays (1280x720: 9 lines at zoom 0.86; 1180x760: 9 at 0.87). The nameplate's faces are preloaded with the look and the nameplate stays hidden (its room kept) until they are in (`g-fonts`, 3 seconds at most).
- **QA:** `GLASS=1 node v2/shot.mjs <tag> v2` shoots the look (it adds `&glass` to the URL); everything else as above. `node scripts/space-check.mjs` checks both looks (LOOKS=glass for one): white space beside stories and drawings, and that no drawing is cropped.

## The QA panel's items (20 Oct 2026, both looks; DECISIONS.md)
Parth: "all as recommended". In `layer.js` unless named.
- **History:** `showDesk(id, to, open, how)`: a tap pushes (`"push"`), Back, Forward and the address (`popstate`, `hashchange`) show without writing (`"pop"`), the first showing replaces; `histMode` holds the rule. In-page jumps (`data-go`, patched in assemble.mjs) keep the desk in the address and land with `land`, which puts the target back under the bar for two seconds if drawings or the live refresh move the page (unless the reader scrolls or presses a key).
- **Keyboard:** `deskFocus` moves the focus to the desk's `h1` (Page One's is `#p1-title`, visually hidden) and names the desk in `#desksay` (polite, visually hidden; class `sr`). "Skip to the news" (`head.html`, `data-skip`) is the first focusable element. `SMOOTH()` gives `auto` under reduced motion, and assemble.mjs uses it for app.js's smooth scrolls too. `headingOrder` sets `aria-level` where a level would be skipped (the look never changes); briefs and "Also in" read as level 3.
- **Page One:** `p1Trim` drops one or two lines of the day in a minute only when that makes the page shorter. Sport this week says IST; `nightOf`/`nightTag` add "Wed night" to a start from 00:00 to 05:59 IST whose time is known (also the Fixture List and Madridismo's table). Money: `closeOf` tags every earlier session's close (`sessionDay`, `goldDay`), each mood shows its own time.
- **Ledger:** `fxNeutral` (the cross whose Yahoo symbol is INR=X) puts the rupee in neutral ink, every period; on phones the open and closed rows show again (a last-column rule was hiding them) and `.calckey` explains the dagger.
- **QA:** `tests/qa-panel.test.mjs`.
- **Round 2 (20 Oct, with the app):** Glass's focus ring is 2px on screen at any zoom (`--z`, set by `setZoom` with the zoom); the desk chips have a dark fill (white 7.8:1 or more on every desk); "Skip to the news" lands the desk title 12px below the pinned bar; both looks: the Ledger's small change figures are a solid `--fig-ink` (#6b675f, 4.9:1 or more). Tests in `tests/pwa.test.mjs`.

## Install (the paper as an app, from 20 Oct 2026; DECISIONS.md)
- **iPhone:** open the paper in Safari, then Share > Add to Home Screen. **Android:** open it in Chrome, then the menu > Install app (or the install prompt).
- It opens in the Glass look: the app starts at `/?glass` (an iPhone's home-screen app has its own storage, so the address turns the look on and the switch remembers it inside the app). Name "The House of 1400", "1400" under the icon; `public/manifest.webmanifest`, icons in `public/icons/` (`node scripts/paper-icons.mjs` draws them from the halftone 1400), the home-screen meta in `public/index.html`.
- **The worker** (`public/paper-sw.js`, built into `dist/paper-sw.js` with its version and shell by `scripts/build.mjs`): network-first for the page, the edition, the config and the art manifests, so the app always shows the newest edition when online and the last copy only when offline. `/api/` is never cached: offline, live blocks show the edition's snapshot with its "as of" line and the bar's live strip leaves it out. Versioned files, fonts and icons are cache-first; a new build is a new worker that deletes the old caches. Not registered on poster links; `/sport` keeps its own worker (`/sport-sw.js`, scope `/sport`).
- **QA:** `tests/pwa.test.mjs` (the manifest, the worker's routing and caches, Sport's scope). The browser checks of 20 Oct (offline, a new edition after a reload, Sport's worker, an iPhone at `/?glass`) served `dist/` over `http://localhost`, where a worker may register; `v2/shot.mjs` and the other QA scripts block workers.
