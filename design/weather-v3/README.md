# Weather, the middle ground (mock, 3 Oct 2026). Nothing here is on the site.

Parth, 3 Oct: "What we have now made is slightly too complex, while it is still beautiful. Can we not find a middle
ground between beauty and design and some degree of cleanliness, and not exactly minimalism?"

Four ways between the slim line of 2 Oct and the ribbon now on the site, each in the whole Weather block (naming
Bengaluru), at 14:15, 17:40, 22:30 and 05:20, laptop or phone width, day or night page: 1 · the line, coloured;
2 · the low arc; 3 · the day's own band; 4 · the hours in dots. The sun and moon are worked out for Bengaluru
(`astro.js`, from design/sunline); the moon is drawn only while it is up.

`node design/weather-v3/build.mjs` bakes `index.html`.
