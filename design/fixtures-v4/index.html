The Fixture List, from scratch

Three ways, each from a different idea, with today's list as it stood at 00:32 on Saturday 10 Oct. Every field is kept. Crests, flags and drivers' photos are the real ones; times, scores and prices are in one scoreboard face throughout.

1 · Broadsheet

A quality paper's listings page, led by type. The time in scoreboard figures; the fixture in the paper's serif with its crests or flags; one typeset line under it for the market or the result (F1 drivers with their photos and team colours). The desk's one colour; the crests bring the rest.
