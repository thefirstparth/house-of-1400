Sky & Streets · round two · real forecasts of Thu 1 Oct · not the live paper

Less rain, more trend, and no rain charts. Behind all three: the forecast the paper reads (the week, today by the hour), ECMWF's 51-run ensemble (two weeks), ECMWF's seasonal forecast (the months, corrected by how far it was off in September), the city's own weather station over 1991 to 2020 for "usual", air quality from the Copernicus service (the last 92 days, the next five, and Octobers to Decembers of 2022 to 2025 for what usually happens), and sunrise and sunset worked out from the calendar. Wind only appears when it is worth a mention; this week it is not (under 15 km/h everywhere). Every sentence is written by rules from the numbers.
